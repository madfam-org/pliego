Paper that folds, turns and pops up.

Pliego is a platform for sheet hyperobjects: from turning a single page to whole interactive paper dioramas — designed as flat sheets, simulated with real paper physics, and made with cut, score and fold files.

Open the studio Why “pliego”?

pliego (Spanish, noun)

A sheet of paper folded in the middle — the printer’s sheet that, folded and cut, becomes a book’s signature.

One fold makes four pages. Everything on this platform starts the same way: a flat sheet and the folds you give it.

Sheet hyperobjects

A sheet hyperobject is a paper object described by its flat sheet — outline, cuts, creases and glue tabs — plus the program that folds it. The 3-D form, the motion and the files to make it all derive from that one source.

  1. Techniquesvalley and mountain folds, reverse folds, scores, tabs and slots — and the page turn, the simplest of all
  2. Origamibases and traditional models, credited to their traditions
  3. TessellationsMiura-ori, Yoshimura, Kresling and twist folds
  4. Kirigami and papercutauxetic cuts, lattices and papercut traditions such as papel picado, with community credit
  5. Paper engineeringV-folds, parallel folds, pull tabs, volvelles and lift-the-flaps
  6. Books and toyssignatures, accordions, tunnel books, flexagons
  7. Dioramaswhole interactive scenes composed from all of the above

Real paper physics

Paper is not cloth and not sheet metal. The engine simulates the stock you would actually use: its stiffness along and across the grain, how a crease remembers being folded, how it springs back, how tissue flutters in the wind while cardstock stands still.

It runs in the browser and in tests alike, deterministically: the same document and the same gestures give the same motion, every time.

  • Grainbends differently along and across the machine direction
  • Crease memorya fold pressed flat and released stays partly open
  • Stock cardsfrom tissue to 300 g/m² cardstock, each with its measured or cited properties
  • Contactlayers stack, slide and rest on the table instead of passing through

Cut, score, fold

Every object comes with the files to make it: cut lines, score lines and fold lines for laser cutters, drag-knife plotters and print, plus the step-by-step folding diagram in four languages.

  • Cut — solid
  • Mountain fold — dash-dot
  • Valley fold — dashed
  • Score — dotted

Part of a family

Pliego is the third hyperobject platform, beside Yantra4D for solid parts and Fashion Cabinet for garments. Hardware such as brads and display stands comes from Yantra4D; book cloth and ribbon come from Fashion Cabinet’s fabrics. Each platform owns its own domain and the others reference it.

Built in the open

The platform code is free software under the AGPL-3.0; the commons of paper objects and stock cards is shared under the CERN-OHL-W-2.0. Pliego is incubating: it is being built now and is not yet a product.
