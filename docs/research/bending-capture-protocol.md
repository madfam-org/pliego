# Panel bending: fixed-24 capture protocol (catalogue bar G1, step 1)

2026-11-11. Committed before the accepted capture. This is the frozen audit named in the
[bending design](bending-domain-next-implementation.md): the accurate path (reference
displacement, UV membrane, two-word arithmetic) on paper that bends out of plane, checked
against an independent linear bending comparator. It is the first of the G1 audits in the
[catalogue acceptance bar](catalogue-paper-acceptance.md); the spine crease, contact and the
hand drive come later, each with its own audit.

## Fixture

- **Patch.** The planar study's cells2 patch: 40 x 40 mm, forward diagonals, grain 0,
  synthetic copy-80 estimate with nu12 = .293. 9 vertices, 8 triangles, 8 flat panel
  hinges. Free: no pins, contact, gravity, damping or drives.
- **Law.** `referenceBending: 'panel-hinges'` (identity `latent-panel-bending` version 2,
  step-scaled angle change), k = C_B·D_eff(phi)·L^2/(A1+A2), flat rest angles.
- **Independent reference** (`bench/bending-modal-reference.py`, standard library only).
  It recomputes every k from the documented law and the represented material words, and the
  flat-state out-of-plane stiffness from piecewise-linear plate kinematics (the slope jump
  across each hinge from barycentric gradients), not from the engine's dihedral. With grain
  0 the stiffness is an exact rational matrix; translation and both rotations are checked to
  be exact null vectors. The generalized eigenproblem with the native lumped masses is solved
  by Jacobi rotations at 90 digits. Engine k agree with the law to 8.8e-17 relative.
- **Mode.** The lowest nonzero mode: omega = 342.859107968530854775 rad/s (54.57 Hz),
  separated from the next by a factor 2.56 in lambda. Scaled to maximum nodal magnitude 1.
- **Initial state.** u_y = A·phi_i (one binary64 product), A = 0.004 mm, the membrane
  study's small amplitude; in-plane u and all velocities exactly zero. One exact
  conversion. The fixture (`bench/fixtures/bending-state-input.json`) pins every word by
  SHA-256 and is regenerated byte for byte by `bench/bending-state-inputs.mjs --extract`.
- **Plan.** The membrane transient rule with z0 = 0.08: S0 = ceil(omega/(60 z0)) = 72,
  h0 = 1/(60 S0), N0 = ceil(2 pi/(omega h0)) = 80, a horizon just over one period
  (18.5 ms). Levels 1/2/4/8 use S = 72·level and N = 80·level; Newton budgets 1/4/16; both
  previous-pose starts. 24 trajectories, 7200 judged steps, 50400 declared Newton
  iterations, plus one restore continuation per row (24 calls, 168 declared iterations)
  outside the judged steps.

## Force policy

F0 = |A|·omega^2·max_i(m_i·|phi_i|) = 11.621186222053531 microN, fixed per row.
tau = max(1e-7, 1e-10·F0) = **1e-7 microN**: the absolute floor applies. Both native gates
use the same tau at every completed step: the latent gate (X0+u) and the public gate
(rounded positions). A latent pass never replaces a public failure.

## Engine arm

One named arm, no tuning and no retry: exact membrane curvature, the half-tau absolute PCG
stop (`pcgAbsoluteTolerance` = 5e-8 microN), Gauss–Newton bending curvature and the
step-scaled bending angle change. These are the planar study's best settings plus bending.

## Runs and comparisons

| Role | Run |
|---|---|
| candidate | `bench/bending-state-audit.mjs --membrane-curvature exact-hessian --pcg-stop half-force-tolerance`, Newton observer on |
| repeat | the candidate again |
| plain | the candidate with `--no-solve-observer` |

- **Strict repeat.** Candidate and repeat must match everywhere except `recordedAt`.
- **Observer parity.** Plain and candidate must match except the named observer fields.
- **Independent force truth.** Every retained completed endpoint role is exported
  (`bench/bending-state-probes.mjs`, no physics) to the 80- and 100-digit oracle
  (`bench/bending-state-precision.py`): membrane truth from the unchanged planar oracle, plus
  bending energy and gradient from exact latent geometry and a high-precision atan2. The two
  precisions must agree outside declared precision fields.
- **Selected Newton review.** Selected full Newton records are reviewed for realized-direction
  descent and real Armijo, now including the bending term.
- **Planar tools.** The only change to a closed planar tool is the Newton observer's identity
  check, which now expects the bending identity on bending Worlds; planar Worlds take the
  same path as before and its planar tests pass. No planar capture is part of this experiment.

Every state and the six native force vectors are retained at every step as Float64LE bytes
with SHA-256. Each observation is re-validated independently
(`bench/bending-state-validation.mjs`).

## Linear comparator

The actual initial field is decomposed onto all nine reference modes and propagated two ways:
continuous (cos/sin per mode) and backward Euler with real M/h^2 per mode. Limits are the
planar study's: backward Euler field and velocity 0.001, energy 0.005; continuous field and
velocity 0.01, energy 0.05, phase 0.01 cycles.

- **Gated scope: out of plane.** The out-of-plane field and velocity (mass norms) and the
  linear energy, bending plus out-of-plane kinetic, against the modal energy. This is the
  linear theory's domain.
- **Reported scope: full field.** Mass norms over the whole 3D field and elastic plus kinetic
  energy, so in-plane motion and membrane energy count as error; also the in-plane mass norm
  and the coupling energy (membrane plus in-plane kinetic). Not gated: see the development
  observations.

This checks the integrator against the represented discrete law at small amplitude. It is
not plate theory, material calibration, or exact propagation of the native rounded
coefficients.

## Development observations before freezing

Development source, the candidate arm, seed off unless stated. Not part of the capture.

- **Force gates.** At budget 16, levels 1, 2 and 8 (880 judged steps): 0 latent failures;
  the public gate failed every step. The public residual sits near 1e-6 microN, the
  rounding floor of positions near 40 mm, above tau = 1e-7. At level 1, budget 1 leaves a
  latent residual near 7e-3 microN (one Newton step from the predictor cannot absorb the
  second-order membrane coupling); budget 4 reaches about 1e-13.
- **Comparator, full field** (as first drafted, budget 16). Backward Euler: field at most
  5.4e-4, but velocity 2.5e-3, 5.1e-3 and 1.9e-2 at levels 1, 2 and 8, and energy 6.61e-3
  at every level. Continuous: field 0.22, 0.12 and 0.031, energy 0.40, 0.22 and 0.061,
  first order in h as backward Euler predicts.
- **Amplitude split** (level 2, budget 16, 60 steps, A = 0.004 / 0.001 / 0.00025 mm):

  | | A = 0.004 | A = 0.001 | A = 0.00025 | Scaling |
  |---|---:|---:|---:|---|
  | Out-of-plane backward-Euler field error | 2.2e-4 | 1.4e-5 | 8.8e-7 | A² |
  | In-plane velocity, relative | 5.1e-3 | 1.3e-3 | 3.2e-4 | A |
  | Energy error | 6.61e-3 | 4.13e-4 | 2.58e-5 | A² |
  | Membrane energy at t = 0, fraction of E0 | 6.61e-3 | 4.13e-4 | 2.58e-5 | A² |

  The energy error equals the membrane strain energy present at t = 0, and the excess
  velocity is in-plane motion. Both are the second-order membrane coupling of an
  out-of-plane deflection, the (A/t)² effect of plates (t = 0.1 mm), which the linear
  reference does not contain. The out-of-plane error itself falls as A²: the integrator
  follows backward Euler on the represented law.
- **Decision recorded before freezing.** A stays 0.004 mm, the approved amplitude. The gate
  moves to the out-of-plane scope above; the first draft's full-field gate is withdrawn on
  this evidence, and the full field stays reported. No limit value changed.
- **Rows through the frozen runner** (the out-of-plane gate, every observation validated,
  all restore continuations passed, every Newton iteration observed):

  | Row | Latent failures | Public failures | Out-of-plane BE field / velocity / energy | Full-field BE velocity |
  |---|---:|---:|---|---:|
  | L1, budget 1 | 80/80 | 80/80 | 3.5e-4 / 4.8e-4 / 4.2e-5 | 2.5e-3 |
  | L8, budget 1, previous pose | 640/640 | 640/640 | 5.1e-4 / 7.7e-4 / 5.6e-5 | 1.9e-2 |
  | L8, budget 4 | 0/640 | 640/640 | 5.1e-4 / 7.7e-4 / 5.6e-5 | 1.9e-2 |
  | L2, budget 16, previous pose | 0/160 | 160/160 | 4.3e-4 / 6.2e-4 / 4.9e-5 | 5.1e-3 |

  Budget 1 fails the latent gate at level 8 too, unlike the planar study where the half-tau
  stop cleared levels 4 and 8: the remainder here is the membrane coupling, not the linear
  solve. Its out-of-plane field still agrees with backward Euler.
- **Cost.** The full-field budget-16, level-8 run took about 8.7 minutes; through the runner,
  budget-16 steps cost about 0.65 s each, mostly precision-limited iterations rejected after
  convergence. A full 24-row run is estimated at 30 minutes.

Nothing in the engine, the fixture, the arm or the limit values was changed after these
observations; the only change is the comparator's gated scope, recorded above.

## Expected reading, not acceptance

- **Latent gate.** Budget 1 fails at essentially every judged step at every level. Budgets 4
  and 16 pass at every step.
- **Public gate.** Fails at essentially every step: the rounding floor of public positions
  (about 1e-6 microN) is above tau.
- **Out-of-plane comparator (gated).** Backward Euler within limits on every row, budget 1
  included; the smallest margin is velocity at level 8 (about 7.7e-4 against 0.001).
  Continuous fails field and energy at every level (backward-Euler damping) and converges at
  first order; phase within 0.01 cycles.
- **Full field (reported).** Above the discrete limits on every row: coupling energy about
  6.6e-3 and in-plane velocity up to about 2e-2 at level 8.
- **Paired controls.** Budget 4 against 16 agree within 1e-4 where both pass the latent gate;
  budget 1 against 4 do not need to.
- **Controls.** Strict repeat and observer parity identical; 24 of 24 restore continuations
  pass.
- **Oracle.** Represented errors within the predeclared limits (membrane and bending energy
  1e-12 nJ; gradient and residual components 1e-9 microN); truth and native gate verdicts
  agree on every sampled endpoint.

Whatever the counts, both force gates and every failure are reported.

## Acceptance limits

Measurement failures and both force failures are retained and reported, never waived because
the comparator is close. Time accuracy stays open. Material calibration, finite rotations,
the spine crease, contact, the hand drive, booklet reading, devices and playback remain
separate requirements. Nothing here merges, deploys or changes Tulana.
